## Known limits / honesty

This is a local mockup. Fixture timestamps are deterministic relative seconds (`id × 0.137`), not a wall clock; the capture tick interval is 1100 ms. TCP data frames are coherent IPv4/TCP segments but do not simulate handshake, retransmission, stream reassembly or a TCP server. UDP connection means an explicit local simulator binding. RX always echoes the payload; DoIP-aware RX response semantics are not promised for arbitrary user inputs. DoIP identification and SOME/IP notification fixtures are inspectable protocol layers only; no UDS workflow.

Display grammar is deliberately small and documented in `#filter-guide`: lowercase protocols and named comparisons joined by `&&`, no OR/parentheses; capture grammar is the four admission choices. Settings/templates persist, packet history/transcript and split geometry do not. Maximum 4 channels, 240 frames/channel, 80 transcript entries, 30 templates, payload 1–4096 bytes. Conversation is retained-packet payload view, not complete Follow Stream. Link UP describes the simulated interface; Stop affects capture only. There is no channel deletion because retained stopped channels are still open and inspectable.
